About Us

Meet our founder

John Foster (commmonly known 'Jo' by his beloved clients) developed a passion for gardening and landscaping by working in his grandparents garden. At the young age of 6 years old, Jo would look forward to seeing his grandparents, Betty and Jo Snr., so that he could learn more about the skills for nurturing a beautiful garden. In 1993, he started a small gardening company called "Forever Gardening and Lawns", which is now better known as Foster Gardens. With over 20 years of experience, Foster Gardens has expanded it's services offeringg to include professional-grade landscaping, design and gardening services into the wider Johannesburg and surrounding areas. Jo continues to be an active part of the organisation and happily nurturers it's staff and customers with as much care as he does his gardens - with sincere and loving memory of his grandparents.

Why Choose us

With a wide range of landscaping and gardening services offered by Foster Gardens, we ensure to make use of the highest quality products, skills and machinery to help ensure that your garden remains your personal haven. The company offers services such as consultations, comprehensive landscape design, installation and garden maintenance. Additionally, Foster Gardens offers personalised plant consultations and treatment plans for its customers.
