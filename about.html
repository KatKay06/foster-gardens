OUR HISTORY

Our founder John Foster developed a passion for gardening and landscaping by working in his grandparents garden. At the young age of 6 years old, Jo would look forward to seeing his grandparents, Betty and Jo Snr., so that he could learn more about the skills for nurturing a beautiful garden. In 1993, he started a small gardening company called "Forever Gardening and Lawns", which is now better known as Foster Gardens. With over 20 years of experience, Foster Gardens has expanded it's services offeringg to include professional-grade landscaping, design and gardening services into the wider Johannesburg and surrounding areas. Jo continues to be an active part of the organisation and happily nurturers it's staff and customers with as much care as he does his gardens - with sincere and loving memory of his grandparents.
