OUR HISTORY

Our founder John Foster developed a passion for gardening and landscaping by working in his grandparents garden. At the young age of six, John looked forward to visiting his grandparents, Betty and John Snr., so that he could learn more about the skills needed to nurture a beautiful garden. In 1993, he started a small gardening company called "Forever Gardening and Lawns", which is now better known as Foster Gardens. With over 20 years of experience, Foster Gardens has expanded it's services offeringg to include professional landscaping, design and gardening services throughout Johannesburg and surrounding areas. John continues to be an active part of the organisation and happily nurtures it's staff and customers with as much care as he does his gardens, in loving memory of his grandparents.
